## About the instructor

<div>
  <p>
  Dr. Ganesh Neelakanta Iyer is a Senior Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science at the National University of Singapore (NUS). He earned his Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science and Engineering from Mahatma Gandhi University (Kerala, India) in 2004, graduating with the University First Rank, before going on to complete both his Master’s and Ph.D. degrees at NUS in 2008 and 2012, respectively. A highly recognized educator, Dr. Ganesh is a multiple-time recipient of prestigious faculty teaching excellence awards, reflecting his commitment to pedagogical innovation and student mentorship.

Dr. Ganesh brings over a decade of rich industrial experience from prominent global technology companies, including Salesforce, Sasken Communication Technologies, NXP Semiconductors, and Progress Software. Throughout his industry career, he excelled in diverse roles such as QA Architect, Technical Support Manager, Lead DevOps Engineer, Software Developer, and Technology Evangelist. Prior to his current appointment at NUS, he also held key academic positions, serving as an Associate Professor at Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham (Coimbatore) and as a visiting faculty member at IIIT-Hyderabad.

A staunch advocate for Agile methodologies, Dr. Ganesh champions the practical application of agile principles in modern software development. Drawing from his extensive experience managing large-scale business products and leading teams as a Scrum Master and Product Owner, he effectively bridges the gap between industry practice and academic theory for his students.

Beyond his academic and professional pursuits, Dr. Ganesh is an accomplished maestro of Kathakali, a traditional Indian dance-drama. He passionately dedicates much of his personal time to preserving and promoting this cultural heritage, frequently organizing and performing in workshops, demonstrations, and full-scale productions worldwide.

To know more about his profile and research, click [here](https://ganeshniyer.github.io/)
