<h2>Showcasing Outstanding Student Work</h2>

<p class="course-note-line">Selected reports may be used as showcase examples in future lectures to illustrate strong testing approaches, clear analysis, and effective presentation of results. All contributing students will be properly credited. Reports will be used strictly for educational purposes within the course.</p>

<h2>Late Submission Policy</h2>

<p class="course-note-line">This policy applies to all submissions in this course. To ensure fairness to all students, <strong>no extensions will be granted</strong>, and you are expected to manage your time appropriately. You are therefore strongly encouraged to submit early to avoid last-minute issues (e.g., illness or technical difficulties on the submission day). The submission page (for reports) will be up on Canvas at least two weeks before the deadline. Submissions are considered late based on the timestamp recorded by Canvas and GitHub.</p>

<div class="callout callout-danger">
  <div class="callout-title">Penalties</div>
  <ul>
    <li>Submission within 24 hrs after deadline: <strong>-50%</strong>. For example, if the assignment is worth 10% and you scored 8%, 5% (half of 10%) will be deducted, leaving a final grade of 3%.</li>
    <li>Submission more than 24 hrs after deadline: <strong>-100%</strong>. No marks will be awarded for your submission.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<h2>AI Usage Policy</h2>

<p class="course-note-line">Students are allowed and encouraged to use AI tools (e.g., for writing code, generating test ideas, or assisting with analysis) for the project. However, all AI usage must be properly acknowledged. A brief statement is sufficient—for example, a single sentence indicating which parts of the work were generated or assisted by AI. No marks will be deducted for the use of AI in the project. The purpose of this policy is transparency, not restriction. Students remain responsible for understanding, validating, and integrating any AI-generated content they submit.</p>

<div class="callout callout-danger">
  <div class="callout-title">Penalty</div>
  <p>Failure to acknowledge AI usage may be treated as improper attribution.</p>
</div>

<h2>Questions and Communication Policy</h2>

<p class="course-note-line">To ensure that questions are addressed efficiently and fairly, please direct your queries as follows:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Lecture content, quizzes, mid-term, and final exam questions:</strong> Post on Piazza, where a dedicated group of TAs monitors and answers questions so that responses are consistent and visible to all students. Do NOT post on Telegram.</li>
  <li><strong>Project-related questions:</strong> For example, implementation details, testing approaches, or clarification of project requirements. Contact your respective Project TA via Telegram or email directly, as they will be responsible for grading your work. Your Project TA is not necessarily your Lab TA — refer to the Project TA allocation in the first lab's slides.</li>
  <li><strong>Lab-related questions:</strong> Contact your Lab TA (Rajdeep) via Telegram or email directly.</li>
  <li><strong>Administrative-related questions:</strong> For example, you can't make it to lab this week because you are feeling unwell. Contact Rajdeep or Prof. Ganesh via Telegram or email directly.</li>
</ul>

<p class="course-note-line">The Telegram group will be primarily used for informal announcements and quick short sharing. We appreciate your cooperation in following this policy, as it helps us support everyone more effectively.</p>

<div class="callout callout-warning">
  <div class="callout-title">Plan Ahead</div>
  <p>In the period just before midterms/exams (e.g., 1–2 days prior), the number of questions posted increases significantly, and we may not be able to respond to all queries in time. Ask questions early to ensure timely assistance.</p>
</div>
