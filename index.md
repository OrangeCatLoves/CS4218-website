   Welcome to CS4218 Software Testing! 
   
   Please familiarize yourself with the module. If you have not read the module overview, now is a good time to do so. The slides for the lectures will be uploaded to Canvas Files one day after the lecture.

In Canvas, you will see a "module" for each week of this class. The information will be populated in these week modules, and they will become visible under the Home section.

---

A summary of the course activities in CS4218 follows. Additional details will be made available during the first lecture.

Lectures will be conducted in person (face-to-face) at the scheduled time indicated in the timetable. Recordings of each lecture will be published most likely the following day. Attendance at lectures is not mandatory. There is a strict quota of 30 students for the face-to-face lab classes. Lectures will begin promptly as per the schedule each week and are expected to last approximately one hour.

This course follows blended learning format. Most lecture sessions will follow a flipped classroom format. I will upload pre-recorded videos one week in advance. You are expected to watch these videos before attending class. During the weekly lecture briefing, we will discuss selected topics from the pre-recorded videos in greater detail and examine related questions and problems to enhance your understanding. We will also have graded weekly quizzes to reinforce the concepts in many weeks.

Labs will be conducted in person.

We would prefer to keep the number of students below 30 in each class so that the TA has time to help with your work during the weeks when we have labs. Labs are not recorded.
However, a specific slot will not be offered if fewer than 12 students register for it. In such a situation, students that register for such a slot will be contacted separately.
CS4218 has a project component. The project has to be completed in teams of five students. It is advisable for all team members to register for the same lab slot, such that you can work together as a team during lab time. However, this is not a strict requirement.
Lab attendance is compulsory and graded. If you are doing an internship and unable to attend the lab in person, then email me at gni@nus.edu.sg with proof of internship from the company, and I will review and waive off the attendance requirements. For those students, there will be a mandatory submission for each lab as an alternate arrangement.
The midterm exam and final exam will be conducted in person (f2f, Examplify, open-book, closed internet). The venue will only be confirmed closer to the exam dates once we have a room allocation.

---

I look forward to meeting you at the first lecture on Mon, 10 Aug, at 2 PM in COM1-0206!

If you have any questions regarding the module, please feel free to email me (gni@nus.edu.sg).
